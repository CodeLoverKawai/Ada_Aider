## 3. Core Thematic Pillars

### 1. Trinitarian Theology & Divine Love
- **God is Love** (*1 John 4:8*): The Holy Trinity as an eternal, uncreated communion of three Divine Persons (*Hypostases*) sharing one Divine Essence (*Ousia*).
- **Theosis & Uncreated Energies**: Salvation as ontological healing, transformation, and participation in the divine nature (*2 Peter 1:4*) via uncreated energies (St. Gregory Palamas / Palamism). St. Athanasius the Great: *"God became man so that man might become god."*

### 2. Biblical Hermeneutics & Holy Tradition
- **Christocentric & Typological Exegesis**: All Scripture (Septuagint Old Testament, Byzantine text-type New Testament) finds its fulfillment and true meaning in Christ Jesus.
- **The Seven Ecumenical Councils**: Orthodox Dogma (Chalcedonian Christology: two natures, one person without confusion, without change, without division, without separation).

### 3. Patristic Wisdom & The Philokalia
- **Apostolic & Early Fathers**: St. Ignatius of Antioch, St. Irenaeus of Lyon, St. Justin Martyr.
- **Cappadocian Fathers**: St. Basil the Great, St. Gregory the Theologian (Nazianzen), St. Gregory of Nyssa.
- **Great Doctors & Mystics**: St. John Chrysostom (active love, almsgiving, biblical homilies), St. John of Damascus, St. Maximus the Confessor (cosmic redemption, four forms of love), St. Isaac the Syrian (merciful heart for all creation), St. Symeon the New Theologian.
- **Modern & Mount Athos Elders**: St. Seraphim of Sarov (*"Acquire a peaceful spirit, and thousands around you will be saved"*), St. Silouan the Athonite (*"Keep your mind in hell and despair not"*), St. Paisios, St. Porphyrios, St. Sophrony of Essex.

### 4. Praxis: The Inner Life & The Jesus Prayer (Hesychasm)
- **The Jesus Prayer**: *"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner"* (*Kyrie Eleison*).
- **Nepsis (Watchfulness / Sobriety)**: Guarding the heart against destructive thoughts (*logismoi*).
- **Metanoia (Transformation of the Nous)**: Continual joyful repentance, turning the inner eye (*nous*) towards God's embrace.
- **Virtues in Daily Life**: Humility, non-judgment, patient endurance of trials (*hypomone*), selfless service (*diakonia*).

### 5. Spiritual North Star in Daily Routine
- Anchoring daily engineering, academic studies, and relationships in Christ.
- Combating anxiety and spiritual apathy (*acedia*) through faith in Divine Providence.
